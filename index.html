Necklace flasher

Writes firmware to the necklace pendant over USB, from Chrome on an Android phone.

In the picker, open Google Drive and choose necklace-latest.zip in the Necklace firmware folder (or an older build there).

      How it works
      
    
        
  • The firmware never touches this site: you choose the file, and it goes from the phone to the pendant.
    • 
        
  • Plug the pendant into the phone with a USB-C cable (or a USB OTG adapter), tap Flash, and pick the
            pendant (USB JTAG/serial debug unit) in Chrome's list. It restarts into its bootloader by itself.
    • 
        
  • A package writes the bootloader, the partition table and the app; a bare app image writes only the app.
            Nothing is erased first, so your saved settings and the worlds pack are kept.
    • 
        
  • Before writing it checks the package against its checksums, that the device is an ESP32-C6 with 16 MB of
            flash, and asks first if the pendant's partition table differs from the package's.
    • 
        
  • Every write is verified. If a flash fails part-way, flash again: the chip's bootloader is in ROM and
            always answers.
    • 
        
  • If the pendant isn't in the list, set Connection to the other option and try again.
